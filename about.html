00 — Who we are

Quality engineered into every fan

Univent has been manufacturing ventilation fans in Egypt since 1979. The range runs from decorative residential units through duct, mixed flow, centrifugal and roof top fans to 400°C/2h certified smoke extraction and tunnel jet fans — 45 products across twelve families, made locally and supplied with test data.

We are also the official distributor for Ruck Germany in Egypt, which puts the full German programme alongside our own production, from one supplier and with one point of technical support.

01 — How we select

CFM is only
half the answer

Two fans can move the same air and still be the wrong choice. We select against the real duty point of your system — not against a catalogue number.

  1. 01

    CFM tells you how much air the system needs.

  2. 02

    Static pressure reflects the resistance created by ducts, bends, filters and dampers.

  3. 03

    The same CFM at different static pressures can require different fan selections.
