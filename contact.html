Before you write

What to include

Three numbers turn an enquiry into a selection on the same day.

Airflow

How much air the system needs, in CFM or m³/h — per fan, not for the whole building.

Static pressure

The resistance created by ducts, bends, filters and dampers. The same CFM at a different pressure can need a different fan.

Application

Residential, healthcare, car park, tunnel or process air — and whether smoke extraction certification is required.
